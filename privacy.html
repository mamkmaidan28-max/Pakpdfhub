Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Pak PDF Hub.

Your files

All PDF and image processing happens locally in your browser. The files you choose are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email address to use the tools. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Advertising and cookies

This website may show ads from third-party advertising networks such as Adsterra. These companies may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and to measure how they perform. They may collect information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party services

To load quickly, the site uses fonts from Google Fonts and software libraries from cdnjs. These services may receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
