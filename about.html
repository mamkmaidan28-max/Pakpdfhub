About Us

Who we are

Pak PDF Hub is a free set of PDF and image tools built for students and everyday users in Pakistan. We made it so that jobs like joining PDFs, shrinking a photo for a form, or turning a CNIC scan into a PDF take only a few seconds.

What you can do here

  • Convert JPG images to PDF
  • Merge PDF files into one
  • Split a PDF into parts or single pages
  • Compress PDF files
  • Convert PDF pages to JPG images
  • Compress images for forms, WhatsApp and websites

Your files stay with you

Every tool runs inside your own browser. Your documents are not uploaded to our servers, so we cannot see them.

Free to use

The tools are free and need no sign-up. The website may show advertising to cover its running costs.
